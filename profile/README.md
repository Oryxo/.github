# Oryxo

We explore ideas at the intersection of AI, research, and technology.
We build research prototypes, useful products, and playful experiments.

Our interests span how intelligent systems learn, remember, create,
and evolve through evaluation, and how they help us discover new knowledge.

## What We Explore

- **Evaluatology** — studying how to evaluate intelligent systems and use evaluation to guide their design, development, and improvement.
- **Memory systems** — helping AI retain, organize, and use knowledge over time.
- **AI for discovery** — using AI to explore questions, generate hypotheses, and uncover new knowledge.
- **Generative AI** — creating and interacting with language, images, and other forms of data.
- **Learning and prediction** — understanding patterns, modeling complex systems, and anticipating what comes next.
- **Products and experiments** — turning ideas into useful tools, interactive experiences, and projects built simply out of curiosity.

These interests are a starting point. We follow questions that seem worth exploring and build things we want to see exist.
